---

App Lock adds an extra layer of security by requiring Face ID, Touch ID, or a passcode when reopening the app after it has been fully closed. It does not trigger when switching between apps in the background.

## How to Enable App Lock

1) Open the Tools tab and tap the Settings button.

{% include theme-image.html
data_light="/assets/images/light/tools_settings_light.webp"
data_dark="/assets/images/dark/tools_settings_dark.webp"
alt="Summary settings button" %}

2) Scroll down and tap on App Lock.

{% include theme-image.html
data_light="/assets/images/light/settings_app_lock_light.webp"
data_dark="/assets/images/dark/settings_app_lock_dark.webp"
alt="Settings app lock button" %}

3) Toggle App Lock on.

{% include theme-image.html
data_light="/assets/images/light/app_lock_light.webp"
data_dark="/assets/images/dark/app_lock_dark.webp"
alt="App Lock toggle in settings" %}

Once enabled, authentication is required only when reopening the app from a fully closed state.
