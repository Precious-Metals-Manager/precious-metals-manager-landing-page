Want to see what dealers are actually charging for gold, silver, platinum, and palladium? The **Offer Prices** view in *Precious Metals Manager* gives you real-time insight into the **retail side** of the market — not just [spot prices](http://127.0.0.1:4000/help/live-spot-prices/).

Here’s a quick walkthrough of how to use this powerful view and what each element means.

---

## 🪙 What Are Offer Prices?

**Offer prices** reflect typical dealer asking prices — what you’d pay if buying from a reputable source. These are different from **spot prices**, which are market-based and don’t include retail markups.

This view lets you:

* Compare **spot vs offer prices**
* Understand **premiums**
* Track **trends by metal**

---

---

## 🔍 What If There’s an Error?

If live prices fail to load (due to internet issues or server delays), you’ll see:

* An **error message** explaining the problem
* A **retry** will happen automatically in the background

You can also manually trigger an update by returning to the screen or adjusting settings.

---

## 🌐 Currency Support

Prices are shown in the currency you’ve selected in the app’s **Settings**. Supported currencies include USD, EUR, GBP, and more.

[Changing the currency](https://preciousmetalsmanager.com/help/change-currency/) will immediately refresh the prices in this view.

---

## 🧠 Why It’s Useful

This view is ideal for:

* Spotting **overpriced** or **underpriced** dealers
* Tracking **market movements** at the retail level
* Making smarter buying and selling decisions
