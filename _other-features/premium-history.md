Spot price you can look up anywhere. What you actually pay is spot **plus the premium** — and the premium moves with demand. **Premium History** in *Precious Metals Manager* charts the premium on a specific coin or bar over time, so you can tell whether today's asking price is a bargain or the worst it's been all year.

> Premium History is a Pro feature. Without a subscription the summary card appears locked and tapping it opens the upgrade screen.

---

## What Is It For?

Use Premium History when you want to:

* Know whether now is a good time to buy a particular product
* See how much over melt value a coin normally trades for
* Compare today's premium against its recent range
* Avoid overpaying during a demand spike

---

## How to Open It

1. **Tap the "Catalog" tab**
   Browse or search the catalog and tap the coin or bar you're interested in.

2. **Find the premium card**
   On the product's detail screen you'll see a summary card with the current premium and a verdict:

   * **Good deal** — the premium sits in the bottom quarter of its recent range
   * **Fair premium** — it's somewhere around average
   * **High premium** — it's in the top quarter of its recent range

3. **Tap the card** to open the full **Premium History** screen.

---

## Reading the Chart

* Pick a window with the **date picker**: 1 month, 3 months, 6 months or 1 year. Only periods with enough data are offered.
* The **dashed line** marks the average premium over that window.
* The **range** under the chart shows the lowest and highest premium recorded.
* **Tap any point** on the chart to read that day's premium, along with the dealer price and spot price per troy ounce behind it.
* The badge at the top turns the current percentile into plain language: *Good time to buy*, *Premium is around average*, or *Premium is high*.

---

## Dealer Prices

Right below the premium card, the **Dealers** card shows how many dealers are offering the product right now. Tap it to open **Dealer Prices**:

* Dealers are listed cheapest first, with the lowest tagged **Best Price**.
* The header shows the dealer count and the full price spread.
* Each row is marked **Live** or **Stale**, with how long ago the price was seen.
* Tap a dealer to open that product's page on their own site.
* Pull down to refresh.

---

## Tips & Notes

* The premium is measured against the **cheapest dealer offer** of the day, so it reflects the best price actually available, not an average of asking prices.
* Prices and premiums are shown in your **selected currency**.
* Premiums are driven by demand, mintage and dealer stock. A low premium on an unpopular product isn't automatically a good buy — it may also be harder to sell.
* A brand-new catalog entry may not have enough history to chart yet. The card appears once there's enough data.
