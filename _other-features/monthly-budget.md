Stacking is a long game, and the easiest way to lose it is to overspend in a hot month. **Monthly Budget** in *Precious Metals Manager* lets you set a spending limit per metal and shows how much of it you've used so far this month.

---

## What Is It For?

Use Monthly Budget when you want to:

* Cap how much you put into metals each month
* Keep your gold and silver buying in the proportions you planned
* Spot a month where you've already hit your limit before placing another order
* Stick to a dollar-cost-averaging plan instead of chasing dips

---

## How to Set a Budget

1. **Go to Tools → Monthly Budget**
   Tap the **Tools** tab, then select **Monthly Budget** under the **Reports** section. The screen is titled with the current month.

2. **Tap "Edit"**
   The **Monthly Limits** sheet opens with a field per metal.

3. **Enter a limit for each metal you want to cap**
   Leave a metal blank to track it without a limit.

4. **Tap "Save"**

---

## Reading the Screen

* Each metal gets a row with the amount **spent of your limit** and a progress bar.
* A row turns **red** once you've gone over the limit for that metal.
* When more than one metal is active, a **Total** row sums the spending and the limits.
* Metals you've switched off under **Settings → Metals** are left out entirely, as are metals with neither a limit nor any spending this month.

---

## How Spending Is Counted

* Only orders with an **order date inside the current calendar month** count.
* Each item contributes its **unit cost × quantity**.
* **Shipping is included**, apportioned across the items in the order by their share of the order's value.
* The month resets on its own — there's nothing to clear.

---

## Tips & Notes

* Budgets are per metal, not per order, so a single mixed order can draw down several rows at once.
* Spending is counted by order date, so back-dating an order moves it out of this month's figures.
* Pair this with the Dollar-Cost Averaging report under Tools → Reports to check that a steady budget is actually lowering your average cost.
