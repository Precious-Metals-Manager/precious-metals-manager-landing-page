---

The concept of "Live Spot Price" in Precious Metals Manager represents the current market price of a precious metal, such as gold or silver, at any given moment. It updates in real-time based on market fluctuations and is crucial for making informed purchasing decisions, or calculating premiums over the spot price.

1) Tap on the "Tools" tab and "Live Spot Prices".

2) Additionally, you can view historical data by tapping on one of the boxes. You can configure the historical data to display prices in "Troy Ounces" (oz t) or "Grams" (g) and choose a time period such as 1 week (1W), 1 month (1M), or 3 months (3M).

{% include theme-image.html
   data_light="/assets/images/light/historical_spot_price_light.webp"
   data_dark="/assets/images/dark/historical_spot_price_dark.webp"
   alt="Historical spot prices" %}

3) You can also add an iOS widget to see live spot prices directly on your home screen. The widget updates automatically and allows you to track the latest market prices without opening the app. To add the widget, long-press on your home screen, tap the "+" button, search for "PM Manager," and select "Precious Metals Manager Widget".
