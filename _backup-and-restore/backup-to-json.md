Whether you're switching devices, creating backups, or sharing data with your accountant, **Precious Metals Manager** makes it easy to export your full portfolio in a structured format.

In this guide, we’ll show you how to export your **entire dataset as a JSON file**, and how to optionally include **attachments** like receipts and certificates.

---

## 🧾 What Is a JSON File?

A **JSON (JavaScript Object Notation)** file is a structured way to store data. It’s especially useful for:

* Creating full backups
* Importing your data into another device
* Sharing your data with a developer or accountant
* Migrating data between apps or formats

---

## 📋 What Does the Export Include?

Exporting to JSON will include:

✅ All your **Orders**
✅ All your **Bullions** (coins, bars, rounds)
✅ All associated **Items** in each order
✅ Your **Sellers**, including tags
✅ Your configured **Tags**
✅ All **notes**, **prices**, and **metadata**

If you choose to, you can **also export your file attachments**, such as:

📎 Scanned invoices
📎 Photos of your bullion
📎 Certificates of authenticity

---

## 🧭 How to Export Your Data to JSON

1. **Go to Settings**
   Tap the settings button on the "Tools" tab or on the sidebar if you are using the app from an iPad or Mac.

2. **Scroll to the “Backup” section**
   This is where you'll find all import/export options.

3. **Tap “Export Data to JSON”**
   Look for the option labeled **“Export dat to JSON”****.

4. Select the location where the CSV file or attachments will be saved.

## 📎 Need to Export Attachments Too?

If you also want to back up your images, scanned receipts, and other attachments:

1. In the same **Backup** section of Settings
2. Tap **“Export attachments”**

Attachments are exported separately to keep file sizes manageable. It’s a good idea to **export your data first**, then your attachments.

📌 *Tip: If you're planning to restore your data later, be sure to **import attachments first**, so the app can link them correctly.*

---

## 🔄 Importing Later?

If you’ve exported your data and attachments, you can restore them anytime:

1. Go back to the Backup section
2. Tap **“Import attachments”** first
3. Then tap **“Import Data from JSON”**

## 🔒 Is My Data Secure?

Yes — your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it. All files are stored **locally or in your private iCloud account**. You control when and how your data is shared.

---

## 🧰 Use Cases for JSON Export

* Transfer your collection to a new phone
* Share data with professionals
* Keep regular backups
* Migrate to a new format in the future

---

## 🎉 That’s It!

You now know how to export and secure your entire precious metals portfolio using the JSON format. Whether you're a casual collector or a serious investor, this feature ensures your records are safe, portable, and future-proof.
